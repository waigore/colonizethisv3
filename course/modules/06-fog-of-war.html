Four Levels of Knowing

PlayerView tracks four visibility levels. Each tile on the map has one.

?

Unknown

You've never been here. No information at all — the tile might as well not exist on your map.

👁️

Revealed

You saw it once, but it's faded. You remember something was there, but not what's there now.

🌫️

Fogged

You know the province exists and whose territory it is. But you can't see inside — no units, no buildings.

✓

Fully Visible

You can see everything — typically your own territory or allies. Units, buildings, everything.

⚠️
Fogged ≠ Empty

A fogged province might hide a massive army. You know it's enemy territory, but you can't see what's inside. That's why the AI can still attack fogged provinces — it might know something you don't.

Screen 4 of 6

Can the AI Cheat?

No. The AI uses the exact same PlayerView that your UI reads from. No back door.

1
AI asks: "What's at tile X?"

The AI calls visibilityForTile(tileX) — the same function your UI calls.

2
PlayerView returns: fogged

The filter says "you know the province exists but can't see units."

3
AI makes decisions with incomplete info

The AI might guess there's an army in there, but it can't know for sure — just like you.

💡
Fairness by Architecture

This is why ColonizeThis is fair. The AI isn't cheating behind a curtain — it's reading the same newspaper you are. When it "surprises" you with an attack, it's because it saw something in its own territory that you couldn't see from yours.

What Prospecting Reveals

Prospecting reveals hidden resources in unknown or fogged tiles. Without it, you can't see what's beneath the surface.

provincesById All provinces, with or without hidden resources
tiles Every tile, including unexplored ones
PlayerView What you know — filtered by visibility and prospecting
prospectedTiles Set of tiles you've scouted — reveals gold, silver, etc.
